Northstar Brain

Northstar Brain is a private knowledge-work tool operated by the Northstar team for a small group of invited users. Its Google Drive connector lets an authorized local operator find, read and create reference files in a designated private Drive folder.

The connector runs on an operator-controlled computer. Company knowledge is maintained separately in a private GitHub repository. This public site contains app information and legal policies only. It does not provide access to the private workspace, company files, OAuth credentials or Google Drive contents.

Google Drive access

The application uses a dedicated Google account and requests Google Drive permission. That permission is broader than one folder; the connector applies additional checks to restrict its supported operations to the designated folder and approved references. The operator should not share unrelated files with the dedicated account.

Access and support

This is an invitation-only internal tool, not a public registration service. For access, privacy requests or support, contact your Northstar administrator using the user-support email displayed on the application's Google consent screen.
